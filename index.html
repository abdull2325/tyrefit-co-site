What Is TYRE-FIT

Everything a professional mobile tyre fitter needs. In one place.

Right now your business runs across missed calls, WhatsApp messages, bits of paper, bank transfers and memory. TYRE-FIT replaces all of it. One app. Your whole working day — from first enquiry to final payment — organised, automated and protected.

Enquiries captured automatically. Quotes sent in seconds. Jobs routed and navigated. Evidence collected on every visit. Payments processed instantly. Reviews requested. 30-day emergency cover activated for every customer. All without lifting a finger.

This is not a booking tool. Not a lead generator. Not a marketplace. It's a complete operating system for your business.

What You Get

Built for how you actually work.

1
Never lose an enquiry again
Missed calls, SMS and WhatsApp all captured and responded to automatically within 30 seconds. Every lead lands on your dashboard — organised, ready to quote.
2
Quotes sent and confirmed in minutes
Job details pre-filled from the enquiry. You enter your price, hit send. Customer gets a payment link. They pay a £5.95 deposit to confirm — no-shows drop to near zero.
3
Your whole route — planned and navigated
Jobs ordered by route automatically. Depot stops included. Sat nav built in. No planning. No back-and-forth driving. Just follow the list.
4
Dispute protection on every job
Before and after photos built into the job flow. GPS-tagged and timestamped automatically. If a customer ever disputes a job — you have the evidence. Every time.
5
Payments, receipts and invoices — automatic
Card, cash or QR. Receipt fires automatically when you mark a job complete. Earnings in your wallet — withdraw when you want. HMRC mileage tracking built in.
6
Google reviews — without asking
Review request fires 2 hours after every completed job — straight to your Google listing. Your reputation builds itself while you're already on the next job.
7
Stock management from the van
Scan tyres with your camera to log stock. Know what's in the van before you drive. No more arriving with the wrong tyre.
